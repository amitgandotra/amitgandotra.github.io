Pattern Recognition Playbook

Most interview problems are a familiar technique in disguise. The skill is not memorising solutions, it is noticing the keywords, data shape and constraints that give the technique away. This page is the cheat sheet for the whole DSA topic: a process to follow, "if you see X, think Y" rules, two decision guides (by input shape and by what the question asks), the pattern catalog, a constraints-to-complexity table, an interactive pattern finder, a Python toolbox, and a drill to train your eye.
